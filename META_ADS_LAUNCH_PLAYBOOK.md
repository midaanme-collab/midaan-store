## 4. UTM Attribution Tracking Checklist

Every click generated by these ads passes UTM parameters that are automatically captured by `midaan.net`:
1. Visitor lands on `https://midaan.net/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=drop01&utm_content=video_sout&fbclid=...`.
2. `src/utils/crm.ts` stores parameters inside browser `sessionStorage`.
3. When the customer confirms their order on WhatsApp, `sendOrderToCrm()` automatically includes `[UTM: src:meta | med:paid_social | cmp:drop01 | cnt:video_sout | fbclid:...]` in the Google Sheets CRM row.
4. Meta Pixel simultaneously triggers the `Lead` event with matching `order_id`, `value`, and `currency`.
